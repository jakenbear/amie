Classified · Eastern Chipmunk Council

The White Devil

To: All burrows, east side of the park
Re: Her. Again.

Fellow chipmunks. She has been sighted again. Pale as a snowbank, silent as a cloud, and she knows which tree you live in. Our elders called her a ghost. Our younger members call her The White Devil. We call her nothing out loud, because she can hear the word “chipmunk” from three fields away.

Intelligence suggests she is almost fourteen. Intelligence also notes she does not move like she is almost fourteen. She has been doing this longer than most of us have been alive, and she shows no sign of retiring.

She does not bark. She does not blink. She simply stares at the base of the tree until someone panics. Do not be the one who panics.

Stay in your burrows. Keep your cheeks full. Trust no white fluff.

— Chief Acorn Officer, on behalf of the Council

Happy Birthday, Lauren

Dear Lauren,

Happy birthday!

Thank you for everything you do for me. Thank you for always giving me cuddles when you walk by (yes, I noticed every single one, and yes, you can keep doing that forever).

Thank you for protecting me, and for letting me live the best life a white devil could ever ask for. Long walks, good snacks, the comfy spot on the couch, and you.

I’m almost fourteen now, and I’ve figured out what matters most: you, snacks, tubes, sardines, and cuddles on my dog bed. In that order. (Mostly.)

I hope your birthday is full of naps, treats and zero chipmunks (unless they’re for me).

Love you lots,
Amie 🐾
